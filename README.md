# Marble Dash

A party game for up to six players: marbles racing on sand, painted in watercolour.
The big screen (computer or TV) shows the game; every phone is a controller.

**Play:** https://pietrodega.github.io/MarbleDash/

## How to play

1. Open the game on a computer connected to a TV or big screen and press any button.
2. Each player scans the QR code with their phone, picks a marble and presses Ready.
3. Pull back on the phone and let go to sling your marble.

Two modes:

- **Race**: first across the line scores 1 point, second half a point. 3 points take the game.
- **Arena**: take turns to hit each other, with your aim hidden. Three hits and you are out; the last one standing wins.

No phones to hand? Choose "Practise with the mouse" and add a few bots.
